# Business Analysis Case Study

## Overview

This repository presents a fictional Business Analysis case study created to demonstrate practical business analysis techniques, process improvement, and requirements gathering.

The project follows a realistic business scenario inspired by common operational challenges faced by growing organizations.

> **Note**
>
> This is an educational portfolio project using a fictional company and synthetic business scenarios.

---

## Company

**NovaRetail**

Industry: Retail

Employees: ~450

Locations: Portugal & Spain

Business Areas:
- Operations
- Sales
- Customer Support
- Finance
- Marketing
- Warehouse

---

## Business Problem

NovaRetail manages internal operational requests through emails, spreadsheets and Microsoft Teams messages.

As the company has grown, this has resulted in:

- duplicated work
- unclear ownership
- inconsistent information
- delayed responses
- limited visibility for managers

Management wants to standardize the process and improve operational efficiency.

---

## Project Objectives

- Understand the current business process
- Identify operational pain points
- Gather business requirements
- Design an improved future-state process
- Define operational KPIs
- Recommend process improvements and automation opportunities
